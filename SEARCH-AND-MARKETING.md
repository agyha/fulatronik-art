# Search and marketing

The website uses https://fulatronik.art/ for canonical page addresses, sharing metadata, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml. Keep the existing domain configuration when publishing.

After deployment, verify the site in Google Search Console and submit https://fulatronik.art/sitemap.xml. Check indexing, search impressions, and clicks. No advertising or analytics trackers are installed.

Artwork information is imported from the spreadsheet into the site. Both galleries use ordinary 2D artwork overviews and labeled Details dialogs. Separate public artwork pages could be added later for individually shareable artwork URLs.

Mailing-list and art-inquiry forms use Google Forms. Opening a form is not a completed submission; review responses in Google Forms. Test the published mobile experience on real phones.
